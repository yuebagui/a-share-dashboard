# Geopolitical Market Risk

## Core Rule

Geopolitical information changes quickly and can include misinformation. Verify current claims with primary or reputable sources before drawing conclusions. Separate:

- Confirmed event: who did what, where, when, with source.
- Policy/legal status: proposed, announced, enacted, enforced, challenged, expired.
- Market channel: supply, demand, logistics, sanctions, tariff cost, technology access, capital flows, risk premium.
- Investment implication: direct exposure, indirect exposure, sentiment exposure.

Do not amplify casualty claims, military movement claims, sanctions lists, or export-control details without current verification.

## Source Priority

1. Official sources: government departments, treasury/sanctions offices, commerce ministries, customs, central banks, UN, WTO, NATO, EU, exchange/regulator notices.
2. Legal and policy text: sanctions lists, export-control rules, tariff schedules, legislation, executive orders, official FAQs.
3. Reputable reporting: Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, Nikkei Asia, The Diplomat, CSIS, IISS, Rhodium, Eurasia Group when accessible.
4. Market data: energy, freight, defense, semiconductor, rare earth, agriculture, shipping, and FX price sources.

## Workflow

1. Pin down the geography, actors, policy instrument, and effective date.
2. Identify affected assets and sectors: oil/gas, LNG, coal, grains, shipping, defense, aerospace, chips, rare earths, EV/batteries, ports, insurers, banks.
3. Check whether the event is new, a re-report of old policy, or an escalation from proposal to enforcement.
4. Map transmission channels:
   - Supply disruption
   - Demand destruction or substitution
   - Cost inflation
   - Compliance and sanctions risk
   - Capex reshoring or localization
   - Safe-haven/risk-off flows
5. For company impact, verify actual exposure through disclosures before naming direct beneficiaries or victims.

## Output

Use this structure:

- Event status: verified/proposed/rumored, with date.
- Key facts: concise bullets with sources.
- Market channels: ranked by likely impact.
- Asset/sector impact: direct, indirect, sentiment-only.
- China/A股 read-through: value chain links and disclosure gaps.
- Scenario table: base case, escalation case, de-escalation case.
- Watch next: official dates, meetings, enforcement deadlines, data to monitor.
