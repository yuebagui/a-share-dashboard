# A-Share Sector Theme Map

## Core Rule

Do not call a company a direct beneficiary unless official disclosure supports the exposure. Classify exposure as:

- Direct: disclosed product/order/customer/capacity/revenue.
- Indirect: supplier, equipment, material, channel, or ecosystem link with partial evidence.
- Sentiment-only: market narrative without confirmed business contribution.

For current themes, verify the catalyst date and source before mapping A-share names.

## Workflow

1. Identify the catalyst: policy, global news, product launch, price move, earnings beat, supply disruption.
2. Build the value chain:
   - Upstream materials/equipment
   - Midstream manufacturing/platforms
   - Downstream applications/customers
   - Services, software, testing, logistics, finance
3. For each listed company, check:
   - Official announcement or annual-report business segment
   - Revenue contribution or disclosed order size
   - Capacity and customer verification
   - Valuation and recent price move
4. Rank names by evidence quality, not just popularity.
5. Highlight where the market story may be ahead of fundamentals.

## Output

Use this structure:

- Catalyst: verified event and source.
- Value chain: upstream/midstream/downstream.
- A股映射: direct, indirect, sentiment-only.
- Leaders vs followers: why each belongs there.
- Verification gaps: what must be checked in filings.
- Trading risk: crowding, valuation, catalyst exhaustion, policy risk.
