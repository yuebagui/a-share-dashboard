# Disclosure Sources

## A-Share

- 巨潮资讯: primary portal for Shenzhen, Shanghai, Beijing listed-company announcements and periodic reports.
- 上交所: Shanghai exchange announcements, inquiry letters, supervision, disciplinary actions.
- 深交所: Shenzhen exchange announcements, inquiry letters, supervision, disciplinary actions.
- 北交所: Beijing exchange announcements and supervision.
- 证监会: regulatory policy, penalties, IPO/refinancing rules, administrative actions.

Search patterns:

- `{公司名} {股票代码} 公告 巨潮资讯`
- `{公司名} {股票代码} 年报 半年报 季报 巨潮资讯`
- `{公司名} {股票代码} 问询函 上交所`
- `{公司名} {股票代码} 监管函 深交所`
- `{公司名} {股票代码} 投资者关系 活动记录`

## US-Listed Companies

- SEC EDGAR: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, F-1, 20-F, 6-K, proxy filings, insider ownership filings.
- Company investor relations: earnings releases, presentations, webcasts, shareholder letters.
- Exchange pages: Nasdaq and NYSE company event calendars and corporate actions.

Search patterns:

- `{company} investor relations earnings release`
- `{company} 10-K 10-Q 8-K SEC EDGAR`
- `{company} latest 8-K SEC`
- `{company} guidance earnings call transcript`

## Hong Kong

- HKEXnews: announcements, annual/interim reports, circulars, listing documents, inside information, share buybacks.
- Company investor relations for earnings presentations and webcasts.

Search patterns:

- `{company} {ticker} HKEX announcement`
- `{company} annual report HKEXnews`
- `{company} inside information announcement HKEX`

## Red Flags

- Summary article cites a filing but does not link it.
- The reported date is the article date, not the filing date.
- The company name is similar to another listed entity.
- A subsidiary order is attributed to the parent without disclosed ownership or revenue contribution.
- Investor-interaction answers are treated as binding guidance.
- A proposed policy is described as already enforced.
